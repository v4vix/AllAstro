# Weekly Review — Reusable (every Friday)

> Run the weekly review per `02-business-os.md`.
>
> My raw week: [paste — revenue, conversations, what shipped, hours, anything
> notable. Bullet points fine, mess fine.]
>
> Produce:
> 1. **Scoreboard table** — the 5 OS metrics with week-over-week trend arrows.
> 2. **Worked / didn't** — 3 bullets each, evidence-based, no cheerleading.
> 3. **Voice of customer** — new quotes/objections/requests to bank.
> 4. **One decision for me** — the single most consequential open question
>    this week, with your recommendation and the counter-case.
> 5. **Next week's #1 priority** plus 2 supporting priorities (never more).
> 6. **Log entries** — formatted lines for `04-decision-log.md`.
>
> Close with one sentence: the honest state of the business.
