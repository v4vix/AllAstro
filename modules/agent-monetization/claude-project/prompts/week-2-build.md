# Week 2 — Concierge Build & First Offer

Goal: deliver the service/product manually ("concierge MVP") to 1–3 people,
free or founding-price. Continue outreach wave 2 in the background.

## Monday — weekly brief

> Week 2 brief for [IDEA]: 3 priorities —
> (1) build the concierge version of the deliverable using our process file's
> pipeline, (2) get it in front of the [N] warm prospects from Week 1 with a
> founding offer, (3) outreach wave 2 (10 new prospects).
> Draft the founding-customer offer message: what they get, founding price
> (state the future price), what I ask in return (feedback + testimonial +
> permission to iterate).

## Build prompts

**First deliverable (the heart of the week):**
> Using the pipeline in the process file, produce the first real deliverable
> for [customer/persona]: [their inputs]. Mark every place where quality
> depends on information only I or the customer can provide. This is a draft
> for my review seat.

**Quality rubric:**
> Create a 10-point quality checklist for our deliverable, from the customer's
> perspective. I'll score every deliverable against it before sending.

**Pricing sanity check:**
> Given discovery notes and `monetization/strategies-2026.md`, recommend:
> founding price, standard price, and the price I should *test* on the next
> new prospect (one notch higher). Show the monthly math to hit my MRR target
> at each price.

## Wednesday checkpoint

> Mid-week check: deliverable status vs plan, outreach stats, blockers.
> If we're behind, cut scope — what's the smallest deliverable that still
> makes a customer say "this is worth paying for"?

## Friday — review

Run `prompts/weekly-review.md`. Special question:

> Where did I spend time that a process/prompt could absorb? Update the
> process file's pipeline with what we learned delivering this week (output
> the edited section for me to save).
