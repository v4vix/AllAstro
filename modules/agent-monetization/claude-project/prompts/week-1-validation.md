# Week 1 — Validation & First Conversations

Goal: 20 outreach touches, 3+ real conversations, 1 clear "I'd pay for that."
No product building this week (landing page copy is allowed).

## Monday — weekly brief

> Run the Week 1 brief for [IDEA]: 3 priorities only —
> (1) finish the 20-prospect list with personalization hooks,
> (2) send outreach wave 1 (10 messages),
> (3) draft the one-page landing copy.
> For each: exact steps, what you'll draft vs what I do, time estimate.
> Then draft the first 10 personalized outreach messages for my review.

## Tuesday–Thursday — execution prompts

**Outreach batch:**
> Here are 10 more prospects: [paste names/links]. Draft personalized
> first-touch messages using the sequence from Week 0. Vary openers; every
> message must reference something specific to the prospect.

**Discovery-call prep (use before every call):**
> Prospect: [name, link, what they said]. Give me a 1-page call brief: their
> likely pain, 5 discovery questions ordered, the objection I should expect,
> and the one thing I must NOT do (e.g., pitch too early).

**Post-call debrief (use after every call):**
> Call notes: [paste]. Extract: pains in their exact words, willingness-to-pay
> signals (strong/weak/none), objections, and update our running "voice of
> customer" list. Suggest the follow-up message.

**Landing page:**
> Draft landing copy for [IDEA]: headline (outcome, not features), 3 pain
> bullets in customer language from our discovery notes, how-it-works in 3
> steps, one price with a founding-customer discount, FAQ (5), and a single
> CTA. Keep it under 350 words.

## Friday — review

Run `prompts/weekly-review.md`. Special question for Week 1:

> Based on reply rate and conversation quality, is the offer resonating, or
> should we adjust WHO we target or WHAT we promise before Week 2? Recommend
> one change maximum.
