# Week 3 — Launch & First Paid Customers

Goal: first paid dollar (if not already), public launch of the offer, and a
repeatable content engine started.

## Monday — weekly brief

> Week 3 brief for [IDEA]: 3 priorities —
> (1) convert warm pipeline to PAID (list every warm prospect with the exact
> next message, drafted), (2) publish the landing page + one public launch
> post, (3) start the content engine: 3 pieces this week from our discovery
> notes.
> Draft everything for my review in one batch.

## Launch prompts

**Close the warm ones:**
> For each warm prospect [list], draft the conversion message: reference their
> specific pain from our notes, the founding offer, a deadline (founding
> pricing ends [date]), and a friction-free next step (payment link / 15-min
> kickoff). No pressure tactics — direct and honest.

**Launch post:**
> Write my build-in-public launch post for [platform]: the problem I kept
> hearing (real quotes, anonymized), what I built, one early result, the
> founding offer, one ask. My voice, no hype. Then 2 variants: one story-led,
> one result-led.

**Content engine (repeats weekly from now on):**
> From our voice-of-customer notes, generate this week's 3 content pieces for
> [platform(s)]: each answers one real question a prospect asked. Format per
> platform. End each with a soft CTA to the landing page.

**Objection handling:**
> Compile every objection heard so far into an objection→response playbook.
> Honest responses only — where the objection is *right* (e.g., "you have no
> case studies"), say what we offer instead (founding price, guarantee).

## Friday — review

Run `prompts/weekly-review.md` + first `processes/monetization-strategy.md`
review. Special question:

> Do we have a paying customer? If yes: extract the repeatable pattern —
> where they came from, what convinced them. If no: diagnose the funnel
> stage that's leaking (list → reply → call → offer → close) and propose the
> single fix for Week 4.
