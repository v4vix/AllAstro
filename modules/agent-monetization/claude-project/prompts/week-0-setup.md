# Week 0 — Setup & Idea Selection (Day 1, ~90 minutes total)

Prerequisite: `03-context-profile.md` is filled in and uploaded.

## Prompt 1 — Idea scoring (run first)

> You have my context profile, the 20 business ideas (process files in
> `processes/ideas/`), and the 15 unconventional niches
> (`niches/unconventional-niches.md`).
>
> Score every idea/niche 1–5 on: (a) fit with my actual skills, (b) fit with
> my hours and capital, (c) speed to first dollar given my risk tolerance,
> (d) recurring-revenue potential, (e) moat/defensibility for a solo operator.
> Weight (a) and (c) double.
>
> Output: a ranked table of the top 10, then a deeper writeup of the **top 3**
> — for each: the exact wedge offer, first customer profile, realistic
> 30/60/90-day revenue, and the single biggest reason it could fail for *me
> specifically*. End with your recommendation and the strongest argument
> against it.

## Prompt 2 — Pick and commit

> I'm choosing: [IDEA]. Challenge me once — steelman the #2 option in 5
> sentences. If I still confirm, then:
> 1. Write the decision-log entry.
> 2. Update my "Current status" block for `03-context-profile.md`.
> 3. From the idea's process file, produce my Week 1 plan preview: the 3
>    priorities, and everything I must prepare before Monday (accounts,
>    tools, lists).

## Prompt 3 — 60-minute validation sprint (same day)

> Before I build anything: design a validation test for [IDEA] I can run this
> week with $0. Give me:
> 1. A one-sentence offer statement ("I help [WHO] get [RESULT] via [MECHANISM] for [PRICE]").
> 2. Where the first 20 prospects hang out (specific subreddits, communities,
>    LinkedIn filters, local channels).
> 3. A 3-message outreach sequence (initial, follow-up, break-up) in my voice
>    — casual, no marketing-speak, asks for a 15-min chat not a sale.
> 4. The 5 discovery questions that reveal willingness to pay.
> 5. The pass/fail bar per the OS validation gate.

## End-of-day checklist

- [ ] Idea chosen and logged
- [ ] Idea's process file uploaded to the Project
- [ ] Prospect list started (target 20 names by Monday)
- [ ] Calendar blocks set for the weekly cadence in `02-business-os.md`
