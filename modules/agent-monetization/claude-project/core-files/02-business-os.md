# Business OS — Operating System File

The rules of the business. Claude: treat this file as binding policy. I edit it
deliberately; suggest amendments, don't improvise around it.

## Mission

Build a profitable one-person AI agent business to **$X,XXX MRR** (set target
in `03-context-profile.md`) using AI agents for execution and me for judgment,
relationships, and quality control.

## Portfolio rules

- **Focus rule:** exactly ONE active idea at a time. A second idea may only
  start after the first is either killed or running on <4 hrs/week of my time.
- **Validation gate:** no building beyond a landing page / concierge version
  until 3 real prospects have said they'd pay, or 1 has actually paid.
- **Kill rule:** if after 4 weeks of real effort there are zero paying
  customers AND zero strong buying signals (demo requests, reply rate >10%,
  waitlist >25), the idea is killed or fundamentally pivoted. Log it.
- **Scale rule:** at first $500/mo recurring, we write the automation plan
  (see `automation/automation-setup.md`) and productize what I'm doing by hand.

## Weekly cadence

| Day | Ritual | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Weekly brief: Claude drafts the week's plan from the active process file | 30 min |
| Tue–Thu | Execution blocks; daily brief each morning | 5 min/day |
| Fri | Weekly review + one decision + revenue-mix check | 40 min |
| Last Fri of month | Kill / scale / pivot checkpoint against the rules above | 30 min |

## Scoreboard (Claude: ask for these every Friday, table them, track trend)

1. Revenue this week ($) and MRR
2. Paying customers (count) + pipeline (warm conversations)
3. Outreach sent / replies received
4. Content or product units shipped
5. My hours spent + estimated token/API spend

## Decision rights

- **Claude decides alone:** research direction, draft structure, task ordering
  within an approved plan.
- **Claude drafts, I approve:** all outbound messages, published content,
  pricing changes, product features, anything a customer sees.
- **I decide alone (Claude advises):** kill/scale/pivot, spending money,
  legal commitments, partnerships.

## Standing constraints

- Solo operator. No hiring. Contractors only for one-off tasks under $200.
- No idea that requires regulatory licensing I don't hold. Legal/medical/
  financial outputs always carry a "not professional advice" disclaimer and a
  human-review step.
- Tools budget cap and hours/week cap are set in `03-context-profile.md`;
  plans that exceed them are invalid.
