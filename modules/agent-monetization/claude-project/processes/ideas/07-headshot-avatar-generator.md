# Process: AI Headshot / Avatar Generator

**Idea #7** · **Monetization:** one-time purchase packs (pay-per-task) ·
**Time to first revenue:** 3–6 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 8–12

## Snapshot

Niche-targeted AI photo packs (e.g., realtor headshots, dating-profile packs,
LinkedIn-for-nurses, team headshots for remote startups). The generic market
is saturated — the play is a niche wrapper with tailored styles, marketing,
and distribution. Claude runs marketing/ops; image models do generation.

**Pros:** viral/shareable output, simple one-time pricing, impulse-buy price
point, clear before/after marketing.
**Cons:** GPU/model costs per order, saturated at the generic level, quality
expectations high, refund requests when likeness misses.
**Mitigations:** niche + bundle (headshots + bio rewrite + banner) to
differentiate; generous regeneration policy priced into margin; upfront
"input photo quality" checklist to reduce bad outputs.

## Pipeline

1. **Product:** define niche pack — styles, backgrounds, outfits appropriate
   to the profession; Claude drafts the style spec and prompt templates for
   the image model.
2. **Funnel:** landing page copy, before/after gallery captions, FAQ,
   input-photo checklist, refund policy.
3. **Order ops:** intake → generation → Claude drafts the delivery email +
   upsell (extra styles, LinkedIn bio rewrite).
4. **Marketing loop:** weekly — 3 niche-community posts, 2 partnership
   pitches (recruiters, resume writers, realtor associations), 1 paid-ad
   test if budget allows.
5. **Review seat:** I eyeball every delivered pack for likeness/artifacts;
   consent + disclosure copy stays accurate.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Headshot week: orders [N], revenue [$], refund/regen requests [paste],
> traffic sources [paste]. Draft: this week's 3 community posts, 2
> partnership pitches, one funnel improvement (pick the leakiest step), and
> the upsell email variant to test.

## KPIs

- Orders · revenue · conversion rate · regen/refund rate · cost per order (model spend)

## Pricing & offer

- $29 basic pack · $49 pro (more styles + bio rewrite) · $99 team/order.
- Partner/affiliate: 20% to resume writers and career coaches.

## First 3 actions

1. Pick the niche where "professional photo" is a purchase trigger (job change, license renewal, listing season).
2. Generate my own pack + 2 friends' as the gallery.
3. Post the before/after in 3 niche communities with a founding discount code.
