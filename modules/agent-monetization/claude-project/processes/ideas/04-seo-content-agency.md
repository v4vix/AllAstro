# Process: SEO Blog / Content Agency for SMBs

**Idea #4** · **Monetization:** monthly retainer (productized) ·
**Time to first revenue:** 3–5 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 10–15

## Snapshot

Monthly SEO content packages for small businesses in ONE vertical (e.g., HVAC,
dental, boutique law). AI does research→outline→draft→optimize; I sell,
review, and own the client relationship. Verticalizing beats generic "content
agency" on both sales and quality.

**Pros:** recurring monthly fees, SMBs pay for outcomes not words, repeatable
playbook per vertical.
**Cons:** SEO changes fast (AI-overview era), results lag 3–6 months, clients
need expectation management.
**Mitigations:** sell "search presence" (blog + GBP posts + FAQ/schema) not
just posts; monthly report showing leading indicators; verticals with local
intent are more insulated.

## Pipeline (per client, monthly)

1. **Input:** client's services, city, competitors, target keywords (built at
   onboarding), and last month's Search Console stats.
2. **Plan:** 4-post monthly calendar mapped to keyword clusters + buying
   intent.
3. **Per post:** SERP-intent analysis → outline (H2s, questions to answer,
   local proof points) → 1,200-word draft in client's tone → title/meta →
   internal links → FAQ schema block.
4. **Extras:** 4 Google Business Profile posts + 2 service-page tweaks/mo.
5. **Review seat:** I verify local facts, prices, service claims with the
   client; no invented specifics ever.
6. **Report:** monthly 1-pager — rankings moved, impressions, leads.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Agency week: clients + status: [paste]. Produce due drafts per pipeline,
> this month's report for [client] if month-end, and 5 outreach drafts to
> [vertical] businesses in [city] whose blogs are dead (I'll supply the list).

## KPIs

- Clients · MRR · posts delivered on time · client impressions/leads trend

## Pricing & offer

- $750/mo (4 posts + GBP) · $1,500/mo (+ landing pages, schema, report call)
- Setup fee $500 (keyword map + content strategy) — waived for founding clients.

## First 3 actions

1. Choose one local vertical I can talk credibly about.
2. Build a sample post + mini keyword map for a real (unpitched) business — the portfolio piece.
3. Pitch 15 businesses in that vertical with the sample attached.
