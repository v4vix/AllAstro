# Process: Niche Newsletter Empire

**Idea #1** · **Monetization:** sponsorships + paid tier (freemium→upsell) ·
**Time to first revenue:** 6–10 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 5–8

## Snapshot

A weekly curated + original newsletter for one tight niche. Revenue from
sponsors (once 1k+ engaged subs) and a paid tier ($5–15/mo) with deeper
analysis. AI excels at curation and drafting; the moat is taste and niche
trust.

**Pros:** fast sponsorship revenue relative to audience size, recurring,
compounding asset, low cost.
**Cons:** audience building is slow at first, content fatigue, open-rate
plateaus.
**Mitigations:** pick a niche with an existing watering hole to fish from;
batch production; paid-tier revenue reduces sponsor dependence.

## Pipeline (weekly issue)

1. **Input:** niche feed list (RSS, subreddits, X lists, journals), last
   issue's stats, sponsor slot status.
2. **Curate:** Claude scans my pasted links/feeds → picks 5 items, one line on
   why each matters to *this* audience, ranked.
3. **Draft:** intro (one sharp opinion, 100 words), 5 curated blurbs, one
   original mini-analysis (300 words), subject line ×5 variants.
4. **SEO/web version:** headline + meta description for the archive page.
5. **Sponsor ops:** track slots, draft outreach to 3 potential sponsors/week
   from companies mentioned or adjacent.
6. **Review seat:** I verify every link, check facts in the analysis, pick
   the subject line, and approve sponsor copy.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Newsletter week: here are this week's raw links/notes: [paste]. Run the
> pipeline: top 5 curated with blurbs, intro opinion, one 300-word analysis
> on the most discussion-worthy item, 5 subject lines, and this week's 3
> sponsor outreach drafts. Flag anything you couldn't verify.

## KPIs

- Subscribers (+/wk) · open rate · paid subs · sponsor revenue booked

## Pricing & offer

- Free weekly issue → paid tier $8/mo (extra deep-dive + archive)
- Sponsor slot: $30–50 per 1k subscribers per issue (raise as engagement proves out)

## First 3 actions

1. Pick the niche where I can name 3 communities I already read.
2. Set up Beehiiv/Substack + landing page; seed 50 subs from my network.
3. Ship issue #1 within 7 days — imperfect and on time.
