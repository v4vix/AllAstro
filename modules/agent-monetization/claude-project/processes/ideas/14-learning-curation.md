# Process: Personalized Learning Curation

**Idea #14** · **Monetization:** subscription ($15–49/mo) ·
**Time to first revenue:** 4–8 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 6–10

## Snapshot

A paid personalized learning service for one skill/career track: each member
gets a weekly brief — curated resources, a practice task, and progress
check-ins — assembled from their goal, level, and available hours. Evergreen
demand; the curation quality and personalization ARE the product.

**Pros:** evergreen, recurring, low delivery cost per member once the
resource library exists, scales like a newsletter but priced like coaching.
**Cons:** curation quality must beat "just ask ChatGPT"; churn when
motivation dips; content maintenance as fields move.
**Mitigations:** the practice task + accountability beats raw content; cohort
starts create commitment; quarterly library refresh process.

## Pipeline

1. **Onboard member:** goal, current level, weekly hours, learning style →
   Claude generates their 12-week roadmap from the track library.
2. **Weekly brief (per member, batched):** this week's 2–3 resources (from
   the vetted library), one practice task with success criteria, one
   reflection question, adjusted by last week's check-in.
3. **Check-in processing:** member replies → Claude drafts encouraging,
   specific responses + roadmap adjustments; flags stalled members for a
   personal note from me.
4. **Library maintenance (monthly):** verify links, retire dated resources,
   add new ones with quality notes.
5. **Review seat:** I vet every resource before it enters the library (I
   actually skim/watch it); I write the personal notes to stalled members.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Learning week: members + check-ins [paste]. Batch-produce all member
> briefs per pipeline, draft check-in responses, and list stalled members
> for my personal outreach. Then: 2 public content pieces from this week's
> best practice task (anonymized) to feed the funnel.

## KPIs

- Members · MRR · weekly check-in rate (engagement = retention) · churn · time per member brief

## Pricing & offer

- $29/mo self-paced track · $59/mo with monthly group call · founding cohort 50% off for life.

## First 3 actions

1. Pick the skill track I'm credibly ahead of beginners in.
2. Build the first 4 weeks of the track library (vetted, not exhaustive).
3. Recruit a founding cohort of 10 from communities where beginners ask "where do I start?"
