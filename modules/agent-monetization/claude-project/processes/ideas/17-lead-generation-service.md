# Process: Lead Generation Service

**Idea #17** · **Monetization:** monthly retainer or pay-per-qualified-lead ·
**Time to first revenue:** 2–4 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 10–15

## Snapshot

Researched, enriched, qualified lead lists (and optionally the outreach
itself) for B2B clients in one niche. AI does research/enrichment/
qualification at scale; the differentiator is relevance and personalization
depth, not volume.

**Pros:** directly tied to client revenue = high value, fast to start,
recurring, results measurable.
**Cons:** data privacy/compliance (GDPR/CAN-SPAM), deliverability if I run
sending, lead quality disputes.
**Mitigations:** business-contact data from legitimate sources only; written
qualification criteria agreed with client upfront (kills disputes); if
sending, client's domain + proper warm-up + opt-out honored instantly.

## Pipeline (per client, weekly)

1. **ICP spec (onboarding):** ideal customer profile as explicit filters +
   disqualifiers + "dream 100" examples.
2. **Source:** I pull raw lists (LinkedIn Sales Nav, directories, event
   attendee lists); Claude structures and dedupes.
3. **Enrich & qualify:** for each lead, Claude synthesizes public signals
   (role, company news, tech stack, posts) → qualification score against the
   ICP spec + one-line "why now" angle.
4. **Personalize:** first-touch draft per lead using the angle; sequences if
   I run outreach.
5. **Deliver:** weekly batch of N qualified leads with scores, angles,
   drafts; monthly quality review with the client.
6. **Review seat:** I spot-check 20% of every batch against the ICP spec;
   compliance rules are non-negotiable.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Leadgen week: clients + ICP specs [ref], this week's raw lists [paste].
> Run enrichment/qualification per pipeline; deliverable batches ready for
> my spot-check. Then: per-client quality trend (client-reported reply
> rates), and 5 pitches to [niche] companies offering a free sample batch of
> 10 qualified leads.

## KPIs

- Leads delivered · client-reported reply/meeting rate · clients · MRR · spot-check pass rate

## Pricing & offer

- $750/mo (50 qualified leads + angles) · $1,500/mo (100 + outreach drafts) ·
  or $15–30/qualified lead. Free sample batch of 10 as the pitch.

## First 3 actions

1. Pick a niche where I understand who buys (ideally matching another idea's vertical).
2. Build one sample batch of 10 leads for a real prospect — this IS the pitch.
3. Send 10 pitches with the sample attached.
