# Process: AI Social Media Management

**Idea #11** · **Monetization:** monthly retainer (productized) ·
**Time to first revenue:** 2–4 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 10–15

## Snapshot

Done-for-you social presence for SMBs in one vertical: monthly content
calendar, platform-native posts, and comment/DM reply batches. AI produces;
I keep voice, approvals, and the client relationship.

**Pros:** genuinely recurring, fast to first client, batchable across clients
once the vertical template exists.
**Cons:** platform algorithm changes, "what's the ROI?" conversations,
low-price competition from cheap tools.
**Mitigations:** sell leads/bookings framing not vanity metrics where
possible; one vertical = reusable hooks and content pillars; monthly report
that connects posts to inquiries.

## Pipeline (per client, weekly)

1. **Input:** client voice guide + content pillars (built at onboarding),
   this week's business news (promos, events, wins), last week's stats.
2. **Calendar:** 5–7 posts across their 2 platforms, mapped to pillars;
   hooks first, then full copy + image/direction per post.
3. **Engagement batch:** Claude drafts replies to comments/DMs/reviews I
   paste in — flagging anything sensitive (complaints, refunds) for my
   personal handling.
4. **Client approval:** one weekly doc; approve-by-Thursday rule.
5. **Monthly report:** growth, engagement, top posts, inquiries attributed,
   next month's focus.
6. **Review seat:** I check every post against the voice guide and the
   client's actual facts (hours, prices, offers) before scheduling.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Social week: clients + their updates/stats: [paste]. Produce each client's
> weekly calendar + engagement batch per pipeline. Then: flag the client with
> the weakest month (churn risk) and suggest one format experiment for them.
> Draft 5 prospect DMs to [vertical] businesses with dead accounts.

## KPIs

- Clients · MRR · posts approved without edits (%) · client inquiries attributed · churn

## Pricing & offer

- $400/mo (1 platform, 12 posts) · $750/mo (2 platforms + engagement) ·
  $1,200/mo (+ report call + review responses).

## First 3 actions

1. Pick a vertical where visual proof of work is easy (restaurants, fitness, salons, contractors).
2. Make a 2-week sample calendar for a real local business as the pitch asset.
3. Walk in / DM 10 of them: "I made this for you already."
