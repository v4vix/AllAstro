# Process: AI Content Repurposing Service

**Idea #3** · **Monetization:** productized service, monthly retainer ·
**Time to first revenue:** 2–4 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 10–15 (scales with clients)

## Snapshot

Clients (podcasters, YouTubers, founders who post long-form) send one piece of
content; I return a week of platform-native assets: LinkedIn posts, X threads,
newsletter section, quote graphics copy, shorts scripts. Classic productized
service — fixed scope, fixed price, fast cash flow.

**Pros:** high retainers, genuinely recurring, near-zero startup cost, obvious
ROI for the client.
**Cons:** client management, voice-matching takes calibration, churn if
quality slips.
**Mitigations:** per-client voice guide, monthly review call, cap at 5–6
clients before raising prices.

## Pipeline (per client, per piece)

1. **Input:** transcript/post + the client's voice guide (built at onboarding
   from 10 samples of their best content).
2. **Extract:** 5–8 atomic insights, each tagged hook-worthy / educational /
   contrarian.
3. **Produce:** 3 LinkedIn posts, 1 X thread, 1 newsletter section, 3 short
   video hooks, 5 one-liners — all in the client's voice per the guide.
4. **QA pass:** Claude self-scores against the voice guide; regenerate misses.
5. **Review seat:** I read everything as the client's audience would; check
   nothing is taken out of context.
6. **Deliver:** batch doc every [day]; client approves in one pass.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Repurposing week: clients and their new source content: [list/paste].
> Run the pipeline per client. Then: churn-risk check (any client whose
> engagement or responsiveness dropped?), and draft 5 outreach messages to
> prospects who publish long-form but post nothing on [platform].

## KPIs

- Clients · MRR · on-time delivery rate · client content engagement trend · churn

## Pricing & offer

- Starter $500/mo (2 pieces/mo) · Standard $1,000/mo (4) · Pro $2,000/mo (8 + strategy call)
- Founding clients: 40% off for 3 months + testimonial.

## First 3 actions

1. Pick 10 podcasters/creators in one niche with good content, weak socials.
2. Repurpose one of their episodes FREE as the pitch ("here's a week of posts from your last episode").
3. Send 10 of those pitches; the free sample is the funnel.
