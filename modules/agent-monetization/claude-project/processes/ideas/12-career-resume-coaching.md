# Process: AI Career / Resume Coaching

**Idea #12** · **Monetization:** packages (pay-per-outcome) + subscription community later ·
**Time to first revenue:** 1–3 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 8–12

## Snapshot

Career services for one profession or transition (e.g., "teachers moving to
corporate L&D", "engineers to management", "new-grad nurses"): resume + LinkedIn
rewrite, targeted application strategy, interview prep. High perceived value
at emotional moments; AI drafts, I bring the human coaching touch.

**Pros:** people pay readily during job stress, fast first dollar, natural
word-of-mouth in cohorts, upsell ladder (resume → package → coaching).
**Cons:** personal touch is the product — hard to fully delegate; seasonal
demand; outcomes not guaranteed.
**Mitigations:** niche transition = repeatable playbook; sell process not
placement ("interview-ready in 2 weeks"); testimonials engine from day one.

## Pipeline (per client)

1. **Intake:** questionnaire + current resume + 3 target job postings.
2. **Gap analysis:** Claude maps their experience to the target role's
   language; identifies missing keywords and reframable wins.
3. **Rewrite:** resume (achievement-formatted, quantified, ATS-sane),
   LinkedIn headline/about, tailored cover letter template.
4. **Strategy:** 2-week application plan — where to apply, who to contact,
   networking message drafts.
5. **Interview prep:** role-specific question bank + STAR answer drafts from
   their real stories; mock-interview prompts they can run themselves.
6. **Review seat:** every fact in the resume is the client's truth — I
   verify nothing is inflated or invented. I do the live coaching calls.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Career week: active clients + stage [paste], inquiries [N]. Produce due
> deliverables per pipeline. Then: this week's 3 content pieces (one myth,
> one before/after snippet with permission, one tactical tip) and 5 outreach
> replies to people posting about job searching in [niche community].
