# Process: Faceless YouTube / TikTok Channels

**Idea #2** · **Monetization:** ad revenue + affiliates + sponsor reads ·
**Time to first revenue:** 2–4 months · **Weekly hours:** 8–12

## Snapshot

Narrated niche videos (listicles, explainers, stories) with stock/AI visuals
and TTS or my voice. AI writes scripts and packaging; revenue from platform
ads, affiliate links, and eventually sponsors. Volume + packaging game.

**Pros:** passive once ranked, AI handles 80% of production, no face needed.
**Cons:** algorithm risk, slow start, monetization thresholds (YT: 1k subs /
4k watch-hours), AI-content policy scrutiny — needs genuine added value.
**Mitigations:** two platforms not one, affiliate revenue before ad revenue,
original angles/research so it isn't spam.

## Pipeline (per video, batched weekly)

1. **Input:** niche, competitor videos that worked (links + view counts).
2. **Ideate:** 10 title/thumbnail concepts scored for click-worthiness and
   search volume; I pick 3.
3. **Script:** hook (first 15s, 3 variants), body with pattern interrupts
   every 45s, CTA. Target length per niche norm.
4. **Package:** final title, thumbnail text + image prompt, description with
   affiliate links, tags, pinned comment.
5. **Assemble:** I (or CapCut/Pictory template) match visuals; TTS or record.
6. **Review seat:** fact-check claims, watch the full video once, verify
   affiliate links, confirm disclosure of AI-generated media where required.

## Weekly brief prompt

> Channel week: niche [X], last week's stats [paste]. Generate the batch:
> 3 video concepts with scored titles/thumbnails, full script for the top
> pick, packaging for all 3, and this week's shorts (3 cutdowns from the main
> script). Note which past video's format we should repeat.

## KPIs

- Videos published · CTR · avg % viewed · subs · affiliate clicks/revenue

## Pricing & offer

- Affiliates from video 1; AdSense at threshold; sponsor reads at ~10k subs
  ($200–500/read); channel itself is a sellable asset (~30–40x monthly profit).

## First 3 actions

1. Pick a niche with proven faceless channels AND affiliate products ≥$30 commission.
2. Make video #1 end-to-end this week to time the real production cost.
3. Commit to 2/week for 8 weeks before judging anything.
