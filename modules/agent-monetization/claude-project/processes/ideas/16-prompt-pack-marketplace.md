# Process: Digital Product Marketplace (Prompt Packs & Agent Templates)

**Idea #16** · **Monetization:** one-time products + update subscription ·
**Time to first revenue:** 1–3 weeks · **Weekly hours:** 5–10

## Snapshot

Create and sell reusable AI assets — prompt packs, agent templates, workflow
kits — on Gumroad/LemonSqueezy/my own site. Fastest possible first dollar;
passive after creation. The catch: generic prompt packs are dead — packs must
encode real workflow expertise for a specific job.

**Pros:** passive after creation, zero marginal cost, compounding catalog,
great top-of-funnel for services (idea #2 in the hybrid playbook).
**Cons:** crowded with junk (buyers are skeptical), marketing IS the work,
prompts get copied.
**Mitigations:** productize workflows not prompts (process + templates +
examples + video walkthrough); free mini-pack as lead magnet; update
subscription creates recurring layer and reason to buy vs copy.

## Pipeline (per product)

1. **Pick:** a workflow I (or a niche) do repeatedly with proven prompts —
   ideally extracted from one of my other active processes.
2. **Build:** Claude structures it into the pack format: overview, the
   process map, 10–20 battle-tested prompts with fill-in variables, 3 worked
   examples with real outputs, troubleshooting section.
3. **Package:** sales page (problem → what's inside → who it's for → FAQ),
   3 launch posts, lead-magnet excerpt.
4. **Launch:** Gumroad listing, community posts, my list.
5. **Iterate:** buyer feedback → v1.1 → "updates included" becomes the
   retention promise.
6. **Review seat:** I run EVERY prompt in the pack myself before launch —
   nothing ships untested. Refund policy honored no-questions.

## Weekly brief prompt

> free), one product improvement from feedback, and score my 3 candidate
> ideas for the next pack by (audience demand × my proof × effort).

## KPIs

- Sales · revenue · conversion rate · email list growth · refund rate

## Pricing & offer

- Mini pack $19–29 · Full workflow kit $49–99 · Bundle $149 ·
  All-access + updates $99/yr.

## First 3 actions

1. Extract my most-proven workflow into pack #1 (use what I already do well).
2. Ship it on Gumroad within 7 days with 3 worked examples.
3. Give 10 copies away for testimonials, then launch publicly.
