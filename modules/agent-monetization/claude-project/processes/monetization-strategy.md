# Process: Monetization Strategy Review

Run every Friday after the weekly review, and any time revenue stalls for 3+
weeks. Companion reference: `monetization/strategies-2026.md`.

## Purpose

Analyze the current revenue mix against the 8 monetization models and propose
exactly ONE experiment for next week. Prevents both under-monetizing and
constant model-hopping.

## Inputs I provide

- This week's scoreboard (from the weekly review)
- Current offers and prices
- Any pricing objections or "would you also do X?" requests heard this week

## Weekly prompt

> Using `monetization/strategies-2026.md` and my current scoreboard below,
> run the monetization review:
>
> 1. **Mix table** — current revenue by model (SaaS / services / templates /
>    usage / white-label / freemium / enterprise / affiliate), $ and %.
> 2. **Health check** — am I over-dependent on one model? Is my cash-flow
>    model (services) funding my scale model (product) per the hybrid
>    playbook? Where am I leaving money on the table given the buying signals
>    I reported?
> 3. **One experiment** — propose a single monetization experiment for next
>    week: hypothesis, exact offer + price, who I pitch it to, effort cap in
>    hours, success metric. It must be launchable in ≤1 week.
> 4. **Price check** — quarterly (or if win-rate >80%), recommend whether to
>    raise prices and to what.
>
> Scoreboard: [paste]

## Rules

- Max one monetization experiment live at a time.
- A model change (e.g., retainer → SaaS) is a PIVOT-class decision → log it in
  `04-decision-log.md`.
- Raising prices on new customers is always fair game; changing existing
  customers' prices requires 30-day notice and my explicit sign-off.

## KPIs

- MRR and % recurring vs one-off
- Average revenue per customer
- Experiment win rate (target: learn something every week, win 1 in 3)
